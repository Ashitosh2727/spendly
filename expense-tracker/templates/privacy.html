Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password. We also collect the expense data you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to operate and improve Spendly: to authenticate your account, to store and display your expense records, and to generate the summaries and breakdowns you see in the app. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time, after which it will be permanently removed from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third parties for marketing purposes. Where third-party services are used to operate the app (such as hosting or infrastructure providers), they process data only as needed to provide the service and are not permitted to use it for any other purpose.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on your account page.
